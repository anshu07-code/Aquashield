# Image Credits and Licences

All evaluation images must be properly credited here. Do NOT add images without verifying their licence.

## Adding images

1. Only use images you own, or images with a compatible licence (see below)
2. Add the image to `data/eval/images/`
3. Add an entry in `data/eval/labels.json` with the source field filled in
4. Add a line below with: filename, source/photographer, licence, URL (if applicable)

## Accepted licences for evaluation images

- **CC BY / CC BY-SA**: Appropriate for hackathon use. Must credit the creator.
- **CC0 / Public Domain**: No attribution required but credit is appreciated.
- **Your own photos**: Full rights. Add your name as the source.
- **NOT ACCEPTED**: CC BY-NC, CC BY-ND, CC BY-NC-SA (commercial/non-commercial
  restrictions that may limit hackathon use), or any image without a clear licence.

## Format

```
[filename] — [credit line]
  Licence: [licence name]
  Source: [URL or description]
```

## Example entry

```
eval_001.jpg — Flooded underpass at Minto Road, Delhi, Oct 2026
  Licence: CC BY 4.0
  Source: Photo by [Your Name], taken Oct 2026
  URL: (leave blank for your own photos)
```
